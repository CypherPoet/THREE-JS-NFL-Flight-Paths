## Paste this prompt

```
Generate a 21-second instrumental hybrid trailer cue. NO vocals, NO lyrics, NO choir — instrumental only. Hans Zimmer meets ESPN data visualization. Layers: sub-bass synth pulse on every beat, ostinato 16th-note strings, modern percussion (taiko hits + processed claps + reversed reverb cymbals), a synthetic brass-like pad. BPM 128. Modern, forward-leaning, sportsbook-energy.

Timing map — hit every beat:
- 0–3s: just the sub-bass pulse and a faint string ostinato.
- 3–6s: ostinato strings come up to full volume.
- 6–9s: percussion layers in, building toward a peak.
- 9.5s: THE HIT — sharp 1-beat drop to just the sub-bass, then everything slams back in with a heavy taiko hit + synth-brass stab. Do not soften this; it's the climactic moment.
- 9.5–15s: the full layered cue rides at peak intensity.
- 15–18s: ostinato thins out.
- 18–21s: return to just the opening sub-bass and a sustained pad, fading to silence.
```
