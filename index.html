    How travel miles are calculated

    Every mile reported here is great-circle (haversine) air distance between stadium coordinates, totalled by walking each team's schedule in chronological order. Consecutive road weeks connect venue-to-venue (the team stays on the road); bye and home weeks anchor the team at its home stadium. Totals are rounded to the nearest mile.

    Distance formula

    Haversine on a spherical Earth with R = 3958.8 mi, implemented in src/geo.js · greatCircleMiles: 

    a = sin²(Δφ/2) + cos(φ₁)·cos(φ₂)·sin²(Δλ/2)
c = 2·atan2(√a, √(1−a))
d = R·c

    Using a sphere rather than the WGS-84 ellipsoid shifts a transatlantic distance by less than 0.3% — well below the precision of stadium coordinates.

    Per-team accounting

    For each team we sort that team's away and neutral-site legs by week and walk them in order, starting and ending at the home stadium:

    • Between two consecutive away weeks the team flies directly from the prior venue to the next.
    • If a home or bye week sits between two away weeks, the previous trip ends with a venue → home flight and the next trip starts fresh from home.
    • Neutral-site / international games count for both participating teams — including the team listed as the home designate. The 49ers' Mexico City "home" game is the canonical example: SF still travels.

    What gets totalled

    Selected miles sums the totals for the active (visible) teams. League total sums all 32 teams. Per-team rows in the sidebar show that team's full-season miles.

    Arcs vs. totals

    The arcs on the globe show each team's home → venue flights as independent visual tracks. The numeric totals reflect the realistic chronological path instead — so summing arc lengths by eye won't quite match a team's total when they have back-to-back road weeks.

    What's not modelled

    • We assume bye weeks are spent at home (typical, not universal).
    • No driving or local transit — pure stadium-to-stadium air distance.
    • Preseason and playoffs are excluded; regular-season only.
